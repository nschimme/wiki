`A very large drinking bowl made of well-worn clay is here.`
`>`**`look at bowl`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This large bowl measuring nearly a foot across is made from some kind <br>of ochre-coloured clay. It must have been baked in the sun for a very <br>long time to make it as strong as it is. The bowl is about four inches <br>deep and has pictures engraved around the outside.

</div>

`It is a large clay bowl.`
`It weighs five pounds.`
