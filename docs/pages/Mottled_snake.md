**Mottled snakes** are aggressive [level](./Level.md) ~10-13
mobiles. They have a poisonous bite, and can
[sneak](./Sneak.md).

`A mottled snake is sneaking here, ready to sink its fangs into flesh.`
`> `**`look snake`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This snake is very hard to see, blending in well with its surroundings. On<br>closer inspection, its scales are a patchwork of varying shades of grey,<br>making it look almost sick.  The size of the snake and its fangs give you<br>ample warning of its threat.

</div>
