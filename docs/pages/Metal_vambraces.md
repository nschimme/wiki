**Metal vambraces** are worn on the [arms](./Arms_items.md). They
provide a large amount of damage absorption and slightly decrease
[offensive bonus](./Offensive_bonus.md). They are worth around 6
[gold](./Gold.md) and 2 silver and are sold by most armourers.

`>`**`examine vambraces`**
<div class="wiki-block">

Several plates of thin metal are attached to each other to form this piece of<br>armour. It covers the complete arm except for the elbow, which is covered with<br>small iron links. Leather strips encircle the vambraces, providing a way to<br>close them around the arm. A couple of metal buckles at the ends of the<br>vambraces enable attachment to other pieces of armour.

</div>
