`A pitchfork has been carelessly left lying here.`
`>`**`examine pitchfork`**
<div class="wiki-block">

The fork consists of a long, wooden handle and a metal cross-bar with four<br>spikes attached to its lower end. Although designed as a farmer's tool, it can<br>nevertheless be quite deadly if used as weapon.

</div>

`>`**`consider pitchfork`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This stabbing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is awful while attacking and awful while defending; its speed is slow, and it inflicts little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs three pounds.

</div>
