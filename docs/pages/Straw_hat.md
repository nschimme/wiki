The **straw hat** is worn on the [head](./Head_items.md) and gives a
small amount of [dodge bonus](./Dodge_bonus.md). It can be bought
from most tailors for around 20 copper.

`An ancient flat straw hat is lying here.`
`> `**`look hat`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This hat is made of pale yellow straw and seems quite old. Its brim is large<br>and gives good protection against sun or rain. The band is made of red cloth<br>and a small, grey feather has been fixed to it.

</div>

`> `**`consider hat`**
`It is a straw hat.`
`It weighs about one pound.`
