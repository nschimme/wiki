**Fine chain mail sleeves** are a lightweight piece of mail equipment
worn on the [arms](./Arms_items.md). They can be bought from some
armourers for around 2 [gold](./Gold.md).

`A fine pair of chain mail sleeves lies here.`
`>`**`examine sleeves`**
<div class="wiki-block">

These sleeves are the result of the work of a skilled craftsman. The rings are<br>forged of pure steel and have a dark, grey colour. They are linked to form a <br>tight mail and thus offer good protection but hinder the arm's movement only <br>little.

</div>

`>`**`consider sleeves`**
`They are a fine pair of chain mail sleeves.`
`They weigh five pounds.`
