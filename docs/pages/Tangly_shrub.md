**Tangly shrubs** are aggressive, [evil](./Alignment.md)
[level](./Level.md) 14 [plants](./Plant.md) found in
[Fangorn](./Fangorn.md).

`A tangly shrub seems to rustle as if alert to its surroundings.`
`>`**`examine shrub`**
<div class="wiki-block">

The plant's long stems try to crawl and expand with the help of the rest of the<br>undergrowth that surrounds it. Its entwining leaves have forked tendrils at<br>their tip, and its twisting mass forms clusters at its growth. Rustling its<br>many leaves, it tries to entangle everything and everyone that happens to<br>pass its way.

</div>
