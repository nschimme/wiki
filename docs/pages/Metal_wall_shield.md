The **metal wall shield** is a rather heavy [shield](./Shield.md)
that grants mediocre [parry bonus](./Parry_bonus.md). It can be
bought from most armourers for about 9 [gold](./Gold.md).

`A metal wall shield rests on the ground.`
`>`**`examine shield`**
<div class="wiki-block">

Crafted with great skill this shield offers you good protection. Thick leather<br>straps have been riveted on the inside to allow a sturdy grip to the handle of<br>this huge shield.

</div>

`>`**`conside shield`**
`It is a metal wall shield.`
`It weighs two stone and one pound.`
