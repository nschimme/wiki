The **grocer** tends to the **Traveller's Shop** in
[Fornost](./Fornost.md). The store is open 8am - 1pm and 2pm - 6pm
and stocks a variety of tools, containers and other items, as well as
[coach](./Coach.md) tickets.

`> A large grocer is here, waiting to buy or sell some goods.`
`> `**`look grocer`**
<div class="wiki-block">

A burly man, who works from morning till night stands over the counter here.<br>Meeting your stare back dispassionately, his only concern is whether you<br>have a ware to sell, or something to buy.
