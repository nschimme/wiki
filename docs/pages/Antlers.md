**Antlers** are the item required to complete the elven trapper's
[quest](./Quest#The_Hunt.md). They can be obtained by
[butchering](./Butcher.md) the [noble
stag](./Noble_stag.md).

<div class="wiki-block">

\> `**`look antlers`**` <br>These enormous palmate antlers have not been taken from an ordinary beast. The<br>massive tines are of a deep-amber colour that slowly coalesces into ivory-white<br>points. Neither blemishes nor abnormalities can be seen; the antlers are<br>perfectly symmetrical in shape.

</div>

`> `**`consider antlers`**
`They are a pair of antlers.`
`They weigh four stone and ten pounds.`
