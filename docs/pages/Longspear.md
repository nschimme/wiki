The **longspear** is a [stabbing weapon](./Stabbing_weapon.md). It
is a reward from the [Bandit's Note](./Quest#Bandit.27s_Note.md)
quest and can also be bought from most weaponsmiths for around 8
[silver](./Gold.md).

`A spear, nearly two yards long, is lying on the ground.`
`>`**`examine spear`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This is an ordinary spear which is about six feet in length. The head of <br>the spear is long and narrow. The butt of the spear is shod in iron to <br>make the spear durable.

</div>

`>`**`consider spear`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a longspear.<br>This stabbing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is average while attacking and poor while defending; its speed is slow, and it inflicts little<br>damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs three pounds.

</div>
