In general, activities which take advantage of game features and the
limitations of the code to produce impossible results or make people do
unlikely things are illegal. For example:

\- If you have stolen someone's equipment, it is illegal to give it

<div class="wiki-block">

 to shopkeepers, innkeepers, etc. who would not ordinarily accept it<br> or keep it from the victim.  (On the other hand, if you are a troll,<br> for example, and have acquired a staff, and want to give it to<br> Grinder, this is ok, as Grinder would probably never give it back<br> to the owner anyway.)

</div>

\- Jumping into inaccessible deathtraps with equipment you have stolen

<div class="wiki-block">

 or looted is illegal unless you actually want to stop playing the<br> character (retire). Using a deathtrap to get rid of cursed eq is<br> also considered illegal.

</div>

\- Misleading emotes, like 'emote leaves to the west', are illegal.

` 'emote begins some strange incantations..." belong to these too.`

\- Activities which seek a result that is otherwise unobtainable due to

<div class="wiki-block">

 current effects on a character in the game are illegal. For example:<br> if you are slept and unable therefore to use 'tell', communicating to<br> a person RL (phone, IM, etc.) in order to circumvent your current<br> inability to tell is patently illegal.

</div>
