**Giant slugs** are aggressive, [evil](./Alignment.md)
[level](./Level.md) 9 mobiles, found in
various locations around [Arda](./Arda.md). They have an acid
attack that can [damage](./State.md) equipment.

`A giant slug is here, oozing around.`
`>`**`examine slug`**
<div class="wiki-block">

The thick, round body of the slug measures nearly three yards in length and is<br>totally covered in slime and ooze. Its colours range from a dirty green to a<br>light brown. The beast has left a trail of slime on the ground around it and <br>its two thin eye-stalks keep searching the area for something or someone.

</div>
