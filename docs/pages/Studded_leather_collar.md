The **studded leather collar** can be worn around the neck. They load on
the [bulldogs](./Bulldog.md) in [Malardil's
Castle](/pages/Malardil), and can also be bought from some
tailors for around 1 silver.

`A leather collar, with heavy metal studs has been dropped here.`
`>`**`examine collar`**
<div class="wiki-block">

The collar is made from a strip of thick leather, which is a little more than<br>two inches wide. Though still extremely supple, the leather is blackened with<br>age, making a stark contrast to the highly polished heavy metal studs which   <br>are set into the collar at one inch intervals. The studs themselves appear to<br>be made from iron, judging by the weight of the collar, and are shaped such   <br>that they present a sharply pointed outer edge.

</div>

`>`**`consider collar`**
`It is a studded leather collar.`
`It weighs about one pound.`
