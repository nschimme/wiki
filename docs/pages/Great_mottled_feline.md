**Great mottled felines** are aggressive [level](./Level.md)
~12-13 mobiles, found mostly in the vale of Anduin.
They can [sneak](./Sneak.md).

`A great feline is stalking here, blending into the terrain.`
`>`**`examine feline`**
<div class="wiki-block">

Sleek, mottled hair covers the strong muscles of this great feline. Her sinewy<br>limbs seem well suited for outrunning prey, and her slender body allows moving<br>gracefully through any terrain. Combined with wide paws and sharp claws, this<br>makes the cat one of the most dangerous predators in these lands.

</div>
