**Celebrindal** teaches [scout](./Thief.md)
[skills](./Skill.md) at [Caras
Galadhon](/pages/Lothlórien). She can be found wandering in the
northern part of the city.

`Celebrindal, Lady of the Galadhrim, is before you.`
`>`**`examine Celebrindal`**
<div class="wiki-block">

A tall lady of the Galadhrim is before you, her long golden hair woven into a<br>thick braid slung behind her back. Her posture is that of an experienced scout<br>and her eyes never rest. Her gaze constantly shifts, taking in everything that <br>can be discerned. Yet, they are deep and profound, the wells of wisdom from <br>countless years. Her hands are those of a lady, fair and delicate, yet hide a<br>strength and skill with the bow unmatched by many a living creature. Still, <br>her clothing is that of a maid to the Lady of Lórien: a fine white dress<br>embroidered with lace and silver thread, encircled by a shimmering silken sash <br>at the waist, and a circlet of golden flowers resting on her head.

</div>
