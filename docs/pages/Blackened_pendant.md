The **blackened pendant** can be worn around the
[neck](./Neck_items.md). It loads on some uruk-hai
mobiles, and it can't be sold.

`A blackened pendant lies on the ground.`
`>`**`examine pendant`**
<div class="wiki-block">

The pendant is made of blackened stone. Its edges are rough and jagged, and<br>it fits easily into a grown man's hand. Upon a closer look it appears that<br>an image of a single white hand has been burnt or printed into the stone. A<br>soft leather string has been attached to it with a metal hook.

</div>

`>`**`consider pendant`**
`It is a blackened pendant.`
`It weighs a few ounces.`
