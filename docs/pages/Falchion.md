`A falchion lies on the ground.`
`>`**`examine falchion`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This single-edged blade is short and very broad, with a small crosspiece for<br>protection. The tip of the blade is curved, so that a large portion of the<br>blade's edge is exposed.

</div>

`>`**`consider falchion`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a falchion.<br>This slashing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is poor while attacking and average while defending; its speed is slow, and it inflicts average damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs five pounds.

</div>
