**Narrow grey boats** are a type of [boat](./Boat.md) found in
[Lothlórien](./Lothlórien.md).

`A narrow, grey boat has been left here.`
`>`**`examine boat`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This narrow, grey skiff is constructed from a species of wood that is notably<br>thin, strong, and buoyant. The individual planks have been put together so<br>skilfully that it is nearly impossible to see the seams between them. It is<br>said that the boats built by the elves of Lórien are unsinkable, albeit<br>wayward if navigated by untrained hands. Several short-handled paddles with<br>broad, leaf-shaped blades lie in the bottom of the craft.
