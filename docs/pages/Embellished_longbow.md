The **embellished longbow** is the hardest-hitting [missile
weapon](./Missile_weapon.md) in the game. It is worth about 19
[gold](./Gold.md) and 10 silver, and is sold in
[Lothlórien](./Lothlórien.md).

`A beautiful longbow, embellished with lines of silver and mithril, lies here.`
`>`**`examine bow`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This ancient-looking six-foot longbow has been embellished and ornamented with<br>lines and glyphs of mithril and silver. The inlaid metals sparkle and shimmer<br>in the light, especially under star- or moonlight. The bow appears to be more<br>a work of art than a tool of war; however, when you draw the string, you<br>realize the bow is quite lethal and capable of inflicting great damage.

</div>

`>`**`consider bow`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is an embellished longbow.<br>Its accuracy is excellent, its speed fast, and it causes massive damage.<br>It weighs four pounds.

</div>
