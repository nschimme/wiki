A **dagger** is a [piercing weapon](./Piercing_weapon.md). Daggers
can be bought from most weapon shops for around 7 silver and also load
on [robbers](./Robber.md), [thieves](./Thief.md) and
occasionally other humanoid mobiles.

`>`**`examine dagger`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This blade is two sided, ten inches long and one inch wide. The hilt offers<br>little protection and the handle is large enough for the hand of a man. It is<br>otherwise a well balanced weapon, useful in close quartered combat.

</div>

`>`**`consider dagger`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a dagger.<br>This piercing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is poor while attacking and average while defending; its speed is very fast, and it inflicts very little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs about one pound.

</div>

## Other

A different item with the same name is required for the [Bree
Weaponsmith](./Bree_Weaponsmith.md)
[quest](./Quest#Bree_Weaponsmith.md). Its description is as
follows:

`> `**`examine dagger`**
<div class="wiki-block">

So ravaged by the passage of time that it has become barely useable in combat,<br>this ancient weapon, perhaps a relic of the First or Second Age, still retains<br>some of its original elegance. The hilt is finely chiselled, and the blade -<br>some thousand years ago - must have been extremely sharp.<br>

</div>
`> `**`consider dagger`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a dagger (worn out, antiquated).<br>This piercing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is awful while attacking and average while defending; its speed is very fast, and it inflicts very little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs about one pound.<br>It looks quite impossible to mend.

</div>
