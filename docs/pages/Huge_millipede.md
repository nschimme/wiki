**Huge millipedes** are aggressive, [evil](./Alignment.md)
[level](./Level.md) 10 mobiles found in
[Fangorn](./Fangorn.md).

`A huge millipede is writhing around here.`
`>`**`examine millipede`**
<div class="wiki-block">

A giant millipede of monstrous size, it writhes around ominously. Huge and<br>black, the segments and legs of this brute seem endless as it twists towards<br>you threateningly. It uses its thick body to coil around its prey, constricting<br>them. Its tough mandibles clack together, seeking warm flesh.
