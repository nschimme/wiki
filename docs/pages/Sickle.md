`A gardening tool that looks like a sickle lies here.`
`>`**`examine sickle`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This crude weapon is more suited for harvesting the wheat, than killing. It<br>is cumbersome, with a six foot long pole and a long curved blade attached at<br>the end. A handle at the opposite end can be grasped for better support during<br>harvest, to effectively swipe through the grass. This extra handle would be of<br>no use to an attacker however, and the weapon is meant to be used one-handed.

</div>

`>`**`consider sickle`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This slashing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is poor while attacking and poor while defending; its speed is average, and it inflicts very little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs a couple of pounds.

</div>
