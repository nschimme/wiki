The **short spear** is a [stabbing weapon](./Stabbing_weapon.md).
It is worth around 4 silver, and is sold by most
weaponsmiths.

`A short spear is lying on the ground.`
`>`**`examine spear`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This short, rough iron spear was crafted without paying much attention to<br>the details, probably during a period of unrest when it was important to<br>forge many weapons quickly. Its ugliness does not make the spear less lethal.

</div>

`>`**`consider spear`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a short spear.<br>This stabbing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is awful while attacking and average while defending; its speed is average, and it inflicts little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs a couple of pounds.

</div>
