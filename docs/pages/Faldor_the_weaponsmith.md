**Faldor the weaponsmith** tends to **Faldor's Fine Weapons** in
Harlond. The store is open 8am - 12pm and 2pm -
7pm.

`> Faldor the weaponsmith is here demonstrating his latest work.`
`> `**`look Faldor`**
<div class="wiki-block">

A short, peaceful elven weaponsmith, more interested in crafting good blades<br>than in their deadly usage. Like all elven craftsmen, he believes that a good<br>item is a beautiful one, and he is just as skilled in decorating his works as<br>making them sharp.
