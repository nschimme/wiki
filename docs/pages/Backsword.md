`A long, single-edged sword lies on the ground.`
`> `**`look sword`**
<div class="wiki-block">

This sword is long and thin, having a slight curve to it. The blade is<br>restricted to one side, the back of the sword being somewhat thickened. The<br>hilt offers moderate protection to the hand that wields it.

</div>

`> `**`consider sword`**
<div class="wiki-block">

It is a backsword.<br>This slashing weapon can be wielded one-handed.<br>It is poor while attacking and average while defending; its speed is average, and it inflicts little damage upon hitting.<br>It weighs three pounds.

</div>
